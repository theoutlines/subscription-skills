# Apple Ads for Subscription Apps 2026 (Adapty) — unofficial skill

Source: Adapty, *Apple Ads for subscription apps 2026* (PDF, gated): https://adapty.io/apple-ads-for-subscription-apps/
Unofficial. Not made or endorsed by Adapty. All data belongs to Adapty.

## How to answer

- Name the metric and its denominator. The report mixes:
  - App Store metrics: TTR = taps ÷ impressions; CR = downloads ÷ taps; CPT = cost per tap; CPA = spend ÷ downloads.
  - In-app metrics: install→trial; trial→paid; install→paid; year-1 LTV; ROAS.
- **"CPA" here means cost per download**, not cost per paying user. Cost per paying subscriber is a separate metric.
- Data comes from apps that use Adapty's Apple Ads Manager. They skew toward subscription apps that run Apple Ads seriously (at least $10K a year per app).
- For CPP and tailored-paywall effects, the report gives **relative uplift**, not absolute rates.
- To judge a market, compare **cost per paying subscriber** with **LTV**. Cheap downloads alone mean little (see Nepal and Algeria).
- If asked about a country or niche that isn't in a top or bottom list, say the report doesn't publish it.

## Methodology

- Period: January–December 2025.
- Scope: **8,000+ apps**, **1M+ ad groups**, **90 countries**, **59 niches**. Niches are custom subcategories inside App Store categories.
- Aggregation:
  - Starts at the ad-group level and rolls up, so a few big spenders don't dominate.
  - Reports medians and percentiles where the distribution matters.
- A niche is included only if it has all of:
  - at least 2 apps
  - at least 200K impressions
  - at least 1,000 downloads
  - at least $10K annual spend per app
- Tailored-paywall uplift is measured **within the same apps**: default paywall vs a paywall matched to keyword intent.
- Niches by category:
  - **Productivity:** AI, AI Chatbot, Generative AI, Notes, Office Suite, PDF Reader, Productivity, Time Planner.
  - **Photo & Video:** AI Art Generator, Photo Editor, Screen Recorder, Video Editor, Video Players, Photo & Video, Photo & Video: Other.
  - **Utilities:** Document Scanner, Recorder, Remote Control, Tools, Tools: Other, Translator, VPN.
  - **Education:** Driver License, Education, Education: Other, Exam Preparation, Study.
  - **Entertainment:** Apps for Games, Entertainment, Entertainment: Other, Personalization, Stickers/GIFs/Emoji.
  - **Health & Fitness:** Calorie & Weight Tracker, Fitness & Workouts, Health & Fitness, Health Tracker, Yoga.
  - **Lifestyle:** Auto & Vehicles, Home & Garden, Horoscope & Astrology, Lifestyle, Religion.
  - **Music:** AI Music Generator, Audio/Music Editor, Music Apps, Music Apps: Other.
  - **Books:** Audiobooks & Ebooks, Books & Reference.
  - **Navigation:** GPS Search & Tracking, Navigation, Navigation: Other.
  - **Travel:** Flight Tracking, Travel.
  - **Social Networking:** Social, Social Account Stats.
  - **Graphics & Design:** Graphics & Design, Poster & Card Maker.
  - **Weather.**

## Five key takeaways (Adapty)

1. **Apple Ads converts installs to paid about 2× better than other paid channels.**
   - Install→trial: **3.66% vs 1.45%** (2.5×).
   - Install→paid: **1.92% vs 0.91%** (2.1×).
   - The gap is biggest in emerging markets.
2. **Cost per paying subscriber varies 28× by country.**
   - 24 markets cost under $20 per payer.
   - Guatemala, Costa Rica and Paraguay are under $14.
   - Nepal costs $362.55.
3. **The most efficient markets are Tier 2–3.**
   - Paraguay, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Indonesia and Brazil give 65–76 paying subscribers per $1,000.
   - The US gives **15**, the UK **19**.
4. **Custom Product Pages matched to intent lift downloads by 23%.** Build them around intent clusters, not one per keyword.
5. **Paywalls tailored to the keyword lift paying users by 76%,** and ROAS by **82% at Day 92**.

| Funnel stage | Default | Tailored |
|---|---|---|

Compounded: 100 installs give **1.76 payers instead of 1** (+76%).
- The install→trial lift comes from matching the paywall to search intent.
- The trial→paid lift comes from the in-app experience matching the paywall's promise.

- The plateau from D14 to D31 is where most teams stop measuring, which under-reports the effect.
- Better-matched users retain longer.

**Case study** (AI video app): Apple Ads keywords were mapped to 3–5 intent clusters, each with its own paywall.
- ROAS went from 64.8% to **126%**, profitable within 60 days.
- Net revenue grew +218% while ad spend grew 63%.
- Paid subscribers grew 3.8×, at less than half the cost.
- Active markets went from 3 to 57 countries.

**Practitioner warning** (Nicole Weiss, Brass Finch): a CPP test raised CR by 20% but cut trials by 20%, because the paywall was still generic. Match the in-app paywall to the CPP.

## 5. Custom Product Pages (CPP)

| Metric | Without CPP | With CPP | Lift |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tap-through rate | 9.72% | 10.90% | **+12.1%** |
| Conversion rate (tap→download) | 63.91% | 70.05% | **+9.6%** |
| Downloads per 1,000 impressions | 62 | 76 | **+22.9%** (+14) |

- The two lifts compound: 1.121 × 1.096 ≈ 1.229.
- Apple allows up to 35 CPPs per app. Build them around **intent clusters**.
- Pick CPPs on revenue, not CR. A CPP converting at 48% can beat one at 55% if it brings payers.

## Errata / inconsistencies

1. **"In high-tier markets like the US, Norway, Iceland and Denmark the lift collapses to single digits"**: Iceland is in the top-20 table at **+188%**. Only the US (+5%, from the website teaser) is confirmed as single digits.
2. **Document Scanner CPA:** $3.73 on the niche CR page, $3.74 on the CPA page.
3. **Scope figures:** the website teaser says 3,210+ apps, 45 countries and 30 niches. The full PDF says 8,000+ apps, 90 countries and 59 niches. Use the PDF.
4. **"Utilities apps own almost 50% of the top 20 niches by TTR":** only 2 of the 20 are in the Utilities category (Remote Control, Tools). Photo & Video (4), Productivity (5) and Lifestyle (3) appear more often. The claim may count "utility-like" apps loosely, so don't repeat it.
5. **"Top three see nearly 3× the TTR of the bottom":** 27% ÷ 11.4% = 2.4×.
6. **"CPA varies 14×":** this fits both CPT ($0.109 → $1.576) and download CPA ($0.18 → $2.54). Cost per paying subscriber varies 28×.
